About mens X machina

An internationally known academic research group at the Computer Science Department of the University of Crete, also affiliated with the Institute of Applied and Computational Mathematics of FORTH.

Research focuses on machine learning, causal discovery, bioinformatics, and AI — with a strong emphasis on biomedical applications.
