Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Causality & Biomedical Informatics

A research group at the University of Crete, founded in 2006 and led by Prof. Ioannis Tsamardinos. We combine theoretical, algorithmic and applied research in causal discovery and machine learning, with collaborations across biology, medicine and industry.

Our Publications

Automated Machine Learning & Biomedical Applications

We develop state-of-the-art AutoML methods and apply them to challenging biomedical problems — cancer screening, diabetes diagnosis, and multi-omics data analysis.

Our Publications

Automated Causal Discovery

We are building software that takes your data and automatically discovers, explains and visualizes the plausible causal relationships, supporting better decisions and root-cause analysis through a natural-language interface.

About mens X machina

An internationally known academic research group at the Computer Science Department of the University of Crete.

Research focuses on machine learning, causal discovery, bioinformatics, and AI — with a strong emphasis on biomedical applications.
