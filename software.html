Featured Resources

BioDataome

Creation of a large database of uniformly preprocessed and disease-annotated genomic and epigenomic data. Currently includes 7,345 datasets spanning 847 diseases, enabling large-scale meta-analysis.

View resource →

HUNT — Cancer-Biomarkers in HUNT

An international discovery study seeking the earliest molecular signals of lung cancer and mesothelioma in blood serum. The consortium brings together five universities in five countries, aiming to develop a blood test for screening smokers and people exposed to asbestos.
